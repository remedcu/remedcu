Focus

Smart-account infrastructure

Safe smart accounts held more than $100 billion in assets when Safe announced the milestone in March 2024. On those contracts I took about 2.7 KB off the singleton's deployed bytecode without changing its behavior and worked on the v1.5.0 release. For the ERC-4337 account-abstraction module I measured what user operations cost in gas.

Formal verification

I write Certora specs next to the contracts they check and update them when the code changes. I have done that for the token locking contract, the Safe core contracts, the ERC-4337 module, Guardrail and Safenet's staking contract and guard.

Security review

Before Safe I audited contracts: 11 published reports in 2020 for Kleros, CertiK and QuillHash, then Solidity and Cairo audits at Nethermind. At Safe I coordinate the third-party audits, fix findings in my own code and publish the audit reports.

Selected work

Safenet: staking contract and Safenet Guard

Safe's transaction-security network: validators attest to a Safe transaction, and a guard on the account checks the attestation onchain.

I wrote the staking contract that Safenet Beta runs on Ethereum mainnet and the Safenet Guard, with Certora specs for both. Certora audited both contracts, and Nethermind audited the protocol in September 2026.

Safe guards: Guardrail, Fiducia and the Policy Engine

Three transaction guards for Safe accounts: a DELEGATECALL allowlist, a delay on new kinds of transactions and a policy engine.

I started all three repositories and wrote most of their commits. Certora audited the Policy Engine in August and September 2026.

Safe token locking contract

A contract on Ethereum mainnet that locks SAFE tokens, with a cooldown between unlock and withdrawal.

I set up the repository, implemented lock, unlock and withdraw, and wrote Certora rules for them. Ackee and Certora audited it before the v1 mainnet release.

Safe v1.5.0 release line

A release of the Safe core contracts, audited by Certora and Ackee.

I added the extensible fallback handler to the core repository and fixed 12 of Certora's audit findings. I also integrated Certora's verification of the migration contract and added the deployment addresses.
